Glitter After Dark

An evening of shimmer, sound, and hands-on art—designed to shine without leaving a mess behind.

Come for the sparkle; stay for the making

On Saturday, October 24, 2026, Glitter After Dark transforms Foundry Hall into a playful workshop and late-night gathering. Come curious, come dressed to dance, or come ready to make something bright with your own hands. Across the evening, artists will explore how reflective surfaces catch light, how a small detail can change an outfit, and how spectacle can be created with materials that do not need to scatter across a room to make an impression. Music and guided creative sessions share the space, with plenty of room to watch, join in, or take a break.

This is an event about glitter in all its meanings: celebration, self-expression, craft, and the work that goes into making an experience feel magical. Our artists will demonstrate wearable design and light-based installations using contained materials and reusable reflective elements. The result is a night with plenty to see and make, while keeping loose decorative glitter out of the venue. You can bring your own ideas, but please leave loose craft glitter at home; the event team will provide the materials used in the workshops.

Shine with care

Glitter products are not interchangeable, and “cosmetic” does not mean every product is suitable for every part of the face. The U.S. Food and Drug Administration explains that color additives must be approved for their intended uses, and that some color additives permitted in other cosmetics are not permitted around the eye area. For that reason, Glitter After Dark will not offer eye-area glitter application. Any optional cosmetic product used at an activity will be labeled for its intended use, applied only as directed, and kept away from eyes unless its labeling specifically permits that use. Guests can enjoy the installations and workshops without using cosmetics at all.

That boundary is a practical control, not a claim that every product or application is risk-free. We will provide clean applicators for any permitted cosmetic activity, will not share applicators between guests, and will ask participants to stop if irritation occurs. The FDA’s guidance on eye cosmetics supports taking particular care around the eyes; guests with sensitivities can ask staff about ingredients before taking part. The workshop team will have product packaging available for inspection, and participation is always optional.

A brighter footprint, by design

Small decorative particles can travel far beyond the place where they are used. The United Nations Environment Programme describes microplastics as a widespread environmental concern, and glitter made from plastic can contribute to that problem when it escapes into wastewater or the environment. Rather than promise that a particular glitter is harmless or “eco-friendly,” we have chosen a simpler control: no loose glitter for decoration or general use inside the venue. Our visual effects come from washable, contained materials, reflective fabric, and reusable light-catching installations. Workshop materials will be collected at the end of each session, and the venue team will check work surfaces before the next group begins.

These measures reduce the chance of materials spreading, but they do not make cleanup or disposal irrelevant. Staff will separate reusable pieces from waste and follow the venue’s waste procedures. If you notice a spill or a damaged container, let a team member know rather than sweeping or handling it yourself. Our approach follows the precaution encouraged by UNEP’s work on plastic pollution: avoid unnecessary releases and keep materials controlled at the source.

Room for everyone in the room

Foundry Hall will use a clearly marked entrance, staffed check-in, and unobstructed routes to exits. Before doors open, the event lead and venue staff will review the room layout, capacity, and emergency procedures; during the event, staff will keep exit routes clear and direct guests if the room needs to be paused or cleared. These are concrete controls for a lively gathering, informed by the UK Health and Safety Executive’s guidance on managing crowds at events. If you need assistance, want a quieter break, or have a question about access, speak to the welcome team. We will share access details and any updates with ticket holders before the event.

Glitter After Dark is for anyone drawn to color, craft, music, or a little theatrical shine. Come for one workshop or stay for the whole evening; there is no pressure to wear glitter, be an artist, or join every activity. Tickets are available through the event booking page. Bring your curiosity, leave loose glitter at home, and help us make a night that sparkles while staying thoughtful about the people and place around it.
